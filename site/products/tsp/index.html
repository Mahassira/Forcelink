Overview

About TSP — Triple Superphosphate

Triple superphosphate is a concentrated phosphate fertilizer. Because it delivers more phosphorus per unit of product than single superphosphate, it is a practical choice where a compact product is preferred.

TSP is supplied in granular form and is typically selected by buyers who want a high-phosphorus product for basal or blended use.

Industry role

Role in the fertilizer industry

TSP is an important phosphate option in fertilizer programmes that prioritise concentrated phosphorus delivery. It is frequently used in blends and basal applications.

Typical applications

Where it is used

General application information. Rates and timing depend on crop, soil analysis and local recommendations.

  • Concentrated phosphorus supply for basal application
  • Blending into custom granular fertilizer programmes
  • Phosphorus supply where a higher-analysis product is preferred
