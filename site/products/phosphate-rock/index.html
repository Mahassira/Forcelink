Overview

About Phosphate Rock

Phosphate rock is extracted from sedimentary and igneous deposits and processed before use. It is the starting material from which phosphate fertilizers such as DAP, SSP and TSP are made.

Because grade and impurity profile affect how rock is processed, buyers usually confirm the relevant technical information with the supplier before placing an order.

Industry role

Role in the fertilizer industry

In the fertilizer value chain, phosphate rock sits upstream. Processors convert it into soluble phosphate products, while some agricultural markets apply ground rock directly where soil conditions make that practical.

Typical applications

Where it is used

General application information. Suitability depends on crop, soil and local agronomic practice.

  • Feedstock for manufacturing phosphate fertilizers such as DAP, SSP and TSP
  • Raw material for phosphoric acid and other phosphate chemicals
  • Direct application in limited agronomic situations, depending on soil conditions
