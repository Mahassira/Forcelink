Overview

About SSP — Single Superphosphate

Single superphosphate is one of the oldest commercial phosphate fertilizers. Because of how it is produced, it also contains sulfur and calcium alongside its phosphorus content.

For soils or crops where those secondary nutrients are also needed, SSP is often considered as a single product that covers several requirements.

Industry role

Role in the fertilizer industry

SSP is valued both for its phosphorus supply and for the secondary nutrients it provides. It is commonly applied as a base dressing before sowing.

Typical applications

Where it is used

General application information. Suitability depends on crop, soil and local agronomic practice.

  • Phosphorus supply for field and horticultural crops
  • Sulfur and calcium supplementation where these nutrients are needed
  • Base dressing ahead of sowing or planting
