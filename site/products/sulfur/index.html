Overview

About Sulfur

Sulfur is an essential plant nutrient, and it is also a central industrial input. Much of the sulfur traded internationally is converted into sulfuric acid.

Sulfuric acid is used in the production of phosphate fertilizers, which makes sulfur an important raw material in the broader fertilizer value chain.

Industry role

Role in the fertilizer industry

Sulfur supports crop nutrition as a secondary nutrient and supplies the acid route for producing SSP and DAP. This dual role links sulfur closely to fertilizer production.

Typical applications

Where it is used

General application information. Suitability depends on crop, soil analysis and local practice.

  • Secondary nutrient supply for crops with sulfur demand
  • Feedstock for sulfuric acid, used in phosphate fertilizer production
  • Soil amendment in suitable conditions
