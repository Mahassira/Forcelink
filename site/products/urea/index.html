Overview

About Urea

Urea supplies nitrogen, an essential nutrient for plant growth. It is supplied as white prills or granules and is one of the most widely traded solid nitrogen fertilizers.

Because its nitrogen content is high, urea is handled as a concentrated product. Buyers typically confirm the required grade and packaging when they request a quotation.

Industry role

Role in the fertilizer industry

Urea is a core nitrogen source in global agriculture. It is commonly used for top-dressing during the growing season and, depending on the crop and soil, as a basal application.

Typical applications

Where it is used

General application information. Timing and rates depend on crop, soil and local recommendations.

  • Nitrogen top-dressing during the growing season
  • Basal application depending on crop and soil conditions
  • Nitrogen supply in blended fertilizer programmes
