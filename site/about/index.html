About us

A focused international fertilizer trading business

FORCE LINK GENERAL TRADING LLC is based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, and was established in 2023. The company focuses on the international trading of fertilizers and related raw materials.

Our positioning

Connecting Global Fertilizer Markets

FORCE LINK positions itself as an international trading partner connecting fertilizer products with global markets. Our role is commercial: matching product requirements with supply, and keeping communication clear between buyers and partners.

Our product portfolio covers phosphate rock, DAP, SSP, TSP, urea and sulfur. Each product has its own information page, and we provide technical details on request.

We are a trading business. We do not describe ourselves as a manufacturer of every product in our portfolio.
