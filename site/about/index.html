About FORCE LINK

Connecting Markets Across Egypt and the UAE

FORCE LINK is an Egyptian international trading company, with branches in Egypt and the United Arab Emirates, including Cairo and Dubai.

We specialize in the international trading of fertilizers and related raw materials, connecting suppliers with buyers across Asia and Europe. Our approach is built on clear product information, transparent commercial terms, and direct communication to support trading opportunities across our markets.

Our Value

We connect buyers with suitable products and sourcing opportunities through clear communication and tailored commercial solutions.

Our Vision

To build trusted, long-term partnerships and strengthen our presence in global fertilizer markets.
