Working with us

A focused team across Cairo and Dubai

FORCE LINK GENERAL TRADING LLC is an international fertilizer trading company with branches in Cairo, Egypt and Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We work across Asia, Europe and South America.

We are interested in hearing from people with experience in trading, commercial work, logistics or administration. If you do not see a role that matches your background, send us your CV anyway.

How to apply

Three simple steps

1. Complete the form

Fill in your details and describe the role you are interested in below.

2. Review the email

Your email app opens with your details filled in, addressed to our team.

3. Attach your CV

Attach your CV as a PDF to that email, then send it. Nothing is sent from this website by itself.

Send your application

Application form

Your application is sent by email to info@forcelink-ae.com. Please attach your CV as a PDF before you press send.

How your application is handled. This is a static website, so the form does not send your details to a server. When you submit, your browser prepares a summary and opens your own email application. You review the message, attach your CV and send it yourself. Please do not include sensitive personal information beyond what your application needs.
