About FORCE LINK

A Dubai-based international trading company

FORCE LINK GENERAL TRADING LLC is an international trading company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, established in 2023.

We focus on the international trading of fertilizers and related raw materials, connecting products with buyers across Asia and Europe. Our approach is commercially direct: clear product information, clear terms and regular communication with the people we work with.
